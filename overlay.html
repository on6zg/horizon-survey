Panorama Overlay

Upload a panorama photo taken near the antenna (any width/height -- doesn't need to be a full 360° equirectangular sphere; a normal cylindrical panorama works too). Then click two points in the photo whose real-world azimuth and elevation you know -- spread them apart in the photo for accuracy -- to calibrate. Two points is what lets this work regardless of the photo's actual angular width/height or lens, since the pixel-to-degree scale is computed from what you enter, not assumed. On a different device than the one you recorded points on (e.g. viewing here on a PC), load the CSV you downloaded from the survey page first. After calibrating, click any green marker to see its exact azimuth/elevation -- useful for checking whether a spike in the line is a real obstruction or a bad reading.
