Panorama Overlay

Upload a panorama photo taken near the antenna (any width/height -- doesn't need to be a full 360° equirectangular sphere; a normal cylindrical panorama works too). Then click two points in the photo whose real-world azimuth and elevation you know -- spread them apart horizontally -- to calibrate. That gives the photo's real degrees-per-pixel scale rather than a guessed field of view. The vertical scale is taken from the horizontal one, which is exact for an equirectangular panorama and means two landmarks at the same height calibrate just as well as two at different heights. On a different device than the one you recorded points on (e.g. viewing here on a PC), load the CSV you downloaded from the survey page first. After calibrating, click any green marker to see its exact azimuth/elevation -- useful for checking whether a spike in the line is a real obstruction or a bad reading.

No data leaves your device or is stored on this server -- your photo and CSV are only ever processed in this browser, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
